Fullerton Plumbing Referral operates as an independent trade directory connecting property owners in Fullerton, California with licensed third-party plumbing professionals. Understanding the legal, contractual, and operational distinctions between a referral service and a licensed trade contractor ensures a transparent, reliable service experience.

Our core commitment is consumer clarity: we provide objective educational resources covering local plumbing systems and municipal codes, while leaving physical diagnosis, estimates, trade execution, and billing to verified independent plumbing contractors.

:::takeaway
Fullerton Plumbing Referral acts as an introduction facilitator, not a plumbing contractor. Homeowners and facility managers contract directly with, and make payments to, licensed trade contractors holding valid C-36 licenses issued by the California Contractors State License Board.
:::

## The step-by-step referral process {#process-steps}

:::steps
Identify the issue | Clarify your plumbing symptom using our online service guides—whether a fixture clog, water heater failure, slab leak, or sewer lateral issue.
Provide address details | Call our intake desk with your Fullerton property street address, ZIP code (92831, 92832, 92833, or 92835), and building category.
Direct contractor connection | Our dispatch team connects you with a licensed independent plumbing contractor whose service area covers your neighborhood.
Written estimate and contract | The contractor evaluates your property in person, diagnoses the issue, and provides a formal written quote prior to starting work.
Direct contractor payment | Once repairs are completed and inspected to your satisfaction, you pay the contractor directly according to their invoice.
:::

## Licensing boundaries under California law {#licensing-boundaries}

Under California Business and Professions Code Sections 7000 et seq., any individual or firm performing plumbing repairs, alterations, or installations where labor and materials total $500 or more must hold an active C-36 Plumbing Contractor license issued by the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB). 

Fullerton Plumbing Referral does not perform plumbing contracting, execute physical repairs, or submit bids for home improvement projects. We do not hold a C-36 license, nor do we advertise ourselves as a licensed contractor. When we introduce a caller to an independent contractor, that receiving contractor must independently satisfy all CSLB requirements:

- **Active License Status:** Verification that the contractor holds an active, unrevoked C-36 license in good standing.
- **Workers' Compensation Insurance:** Mandatory coverage protecting all technicians and apprentices working on the homeowner's premises.
- **Contractor Surety Bond:** A mandatory $25,000 contractor license bond on file with the CSLB protecting California consumers.
- **Municipal Permits:** Responsibility for pulling all necessary plumbing, gas, and mechanical permits through the City of Fullerton Building & Safety division.

## Contractor compensation and billing transparency {#compensation-disclosure}

Fullerton Plumbing Referral does not charge property owners for directory access or telephone introductions. Our operational costs are funded through standard directory referral arrangements with participating independent contractors. 

Contractors pay an administrative marketing fee or modest per-referral introduction charge to participate in our network. This fee is an ordinary business overhead expense and does not inflate your project estimate. You contract with the plumber under their standard published rate sheets, and you pay them directly upon job completion. We never collect homeowner credit card numbers or process repair payments.

:::faq
Q: Does Fullerton Plumbing Referral guarantee the work done by a referred plumber?
A: No. All diagnostic evaluations, workmanship warranties, equipment warranties, and liability protections are issued directly by the licensed plumbing contractor you hire. We recommend requesting written warranty terms from your contractor before work begins.

Q: Can a referral service pull city permits for my project?
A: No. Under California law, municipal building permits can only be pulled by the property owner (as owner-builder) or by a licensed contractor holding the appropriate classification (such as C-36 Plumbing or Class B General Building). The contractor you hire will pull permits through the City of Fullerton Building & Safety division.

Q: How can I verify a plumber's California contractor license?
A: You can verify any contractor's license status, bond validity, and workers' compensation insurance in real time on the California CSLB website (cslb.ca.gov) or by calling the CSLB automated license verification line at (800) 321-CSLB.
:::
