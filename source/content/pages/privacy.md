Fullerton Plumbing Referral respects the privacy rights of California property owners. This policy outlines how information is gathered, utilized, and protected when you browse our educational guides or place a telephone inquiry for plumbing contractor referral services in Fullerton, California.

Our site architecture is purposefully built without online lead capture forms, newsletter popups, gated PDF downloads, or third-party ad retargeting pixels. We do not sell personal data, trade contact lists with telemarketers, or maintain customer behavioral tracking profiles.

## Information collected during telephone interactions {#phone-intake-data}

When you contact our referral telephone line, information is collected solely to facilitate a contractor referral:

- **Caller Contact Information:** Your telephone caller ID, full name, and the physical street address of the property requiring plumbing service.
- **Service Request Details:** Information describing the plumbing symptom (such as drain backups, slab leak concerns, water heater malfunctions, or remodeling rough-ins) and property category (single-family, rental duplex, commercial unit).
- **Call Detail Records (CDR):** Basic telecommunications metadata including time of call, call duration, and referral transfer status, used solely for operational quality assurance and dispatch auditing.

We do not record telephone calls without clear automated voice disclosures prior to connection. If live call recording is implemented for training purposes, callers are notified immediately upon dialing.

## Information sharing and third-party contractors {#contractor-sharing}

When a caller requests a contractor introduction, necessary intake information (service address, property contact, and reported plumbing issue) is transmitted directly to an independent licensed trade contractor serving the Fullerton area. Once your call is connected or transferred, that contractor's private customer data and privacy policies govern subsequent interactions, contracts, and payment processing.

We never distribute caller information to lead auctions, affiliate marketing rings, or unrelated commercial vendors. Information transmitted to local contractors is restricted to the specific plumbing job requested by the caller.

## Website analytics and cookie usage {#website-analytics}

Our public static website does not execute behavioral tracking cookies, persistent cross-site tracking scripts, or invasive browser fingerprinting. Basic web server access logs record anonymous IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and visited page URLs solely to evaluate technical performance, identify broken links, and mitigate automated server attacks.

California residents may exercise statutory privacy rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know what personal data has been collected and the right to request data deletion, by contacting our administrative desk.

:::faq
Q: Does Fullerton Plumbing Referral sell caller phone numbers to multiple plumbers?
A: No. We do not operate a shared lead auction model where multiple contractors receive your number and call you simultaneously. Each verified referral call connects directly to one independent plumbing contractor selected based on geographic proximity, licensing, and trade scope.

Q: How long are telephone call detail records retained?
A: Telecommunications records (such as call timestamps, originating phone numbers, and call duration) are retained for administrative accounting and referral dispute auditing for up to twelve months, after which they are systematically purged.

Q: Can I request deletion of my service address and telephone record?
A: Yes. California property owners may request the deletion of non-contractual personal contact records from our directory databases by contacting our administrative privacy coordinator.
:::
