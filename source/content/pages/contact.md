## What to prepare before placing your call {#preparing-for-intake}

To ensure the fastest possible contractor match, please gather a few basic property details before contacting our referral desk:

- **Specific Address and Community:** Provide your Fullerton street address, including your neighborhood (such as Sunny Hills, Amerige Heights, Raymond Hills, or Downtown Fullerton) and your ZIP code (92831, 92832, 92833, or 92835).
- **Property Type and Access:** Clarify whether the location is a detached single-family residence, a condominium with an HOA, an apartment building, or a commercial establishment. In multi-tenant buildings, confirm who holds legal authority to authorize main shutoffs or exterior cleanout excavation.
- **Plumbing Symptoms and History:** Note whether the problem involves one fixture or multiple drains simultaneously. If chemical drain cleaners were recently poured into the line, inform the intake dispatcher immediately so field technicians can take proper safety precautions against chemical burns.

## Emergency municipal and utility contact numbers {#utility-emergencies}

This referral website is intended for private property plumbing services that can be scheduled and quoted by an independent trade contractor. If you face an immediate public utility emergency, contact the responsible public agencies directly:

- **Suspected Natural Gas Leaks:** If you detect a rotten-egg odor or hear hissing near gas meters or underground piping, evacuate the building immediately. Do not flip light switches, light matches, or use cell phones in the vicinity. From a safe location downwind, call Southern California Gas Company (SoCalGas) emergency dispatch at [(800) 427-2200](tel:+18004272200) or dial 911.
- **Municipal Water Main Breaks or Street Flooding:** For major water line breaks in public streets, broken fire hydrants, or municipal sewer main surcharges, call the City of Fullerton Water Division / Public Works emergency standby at [(714) 738-6897](tel:+17147386897). For routine public works questions during business hours, call [(714) 738-6890](tel:+17147386890).
- **Electrical Outages and Downed Power Lines:** For electrical supply issues impacting water heaters or pumps, contact Southern California Edison (SCE) at [(800) 655-4555](tel:+18006554555).
- **Underground Utility Markout:** Always call 811 (DigAlert) at [811](tel:811) at least two business days prior to any yard excavation, sewer trenching, or water main replacement.

Q: Can Fullerton Plumbing Referral provide an exact cost estimate over the phone?
A: No. Because our referral desk does not perform contracting work, we cannot quote repair fees. Plumbing costs depend on physical access, pipe material, code compliance, and diagnostic findings. All pricing is determined by the independent licensed contractor who inspects the property.

Q: How quickly can a referred plumber arrive at my Fullerton property?
A: Arrival windows depend on contractor scheduling, current demand in North Orange County, and the urgency of the problem. Active leaks and complete sewer stoppages receive expedited routing priority from contractors when technician capacity permits.

Q: Does Fullerton Plumbing Referral dispatch city municipal workers?
A: No. We are a private referral service. For issues involving the public water distribution main, public fire hydrants, or municipal sewer mains beneath city streets, residents must contact City of Fullerton Public Works directly.
:::
