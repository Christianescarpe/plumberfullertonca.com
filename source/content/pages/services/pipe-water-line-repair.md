The pressurized potable water supply system delivers fresh municipal water to every faucet, showerhead, water heater, and appliance throughout your Fullerton home. When aging galvanized pipes restrict water flow with rust encrustation, copper lines develop pinhole leaks from water velocity erosion, or underground main supply lines fracture in shifting yard soil, timely repair or whole-house repiping is necessary to protect your property investment.

:::takeaway
For aging galvanized iron pipes or recurring copper pinhole leaks, whole-home repiping using durable Type L copper or flexible cross-linked polyethylene (PEX) offers a permanent, code-compliant solution that restores full household water pressure and prevents water damage.

## Pipe Replacements & Repairs {#pipe-replacements-repairs}

Many Fullerton residences constructed between the 1920s and late 1960s in neighborhoods like [Downtown Fullerton](/areas/downtown-central-fullerton/) or [Raymond Hills](/areas/raymond-hills-golden-hill/) were originally piped with threaded galvanized iron water lines. Over decades of service, mineral deposits and internal rust narrow the interior pipe diameter to a fraction of an inch, severely constricting water volume and producing reddish, discolored water at morning tap openings. 

While an isolated, exposed pipe leak can occasionally be resolved with a targeted copper sleeve or mechanical clamp repair, recurring pinhole leaks or systemic rust require Pipe Replacements & Repairs. Licensed plumbing contractors evaluate whole-house repiping utilizing two modern industry standards:

- **Type L Hard Copper:** Renowned for exceptional rigidity, ultraviolet resistance, and multi-decade durability. Copper is non-permeable, naturally antimicrobial, and widely preferred for exposed mechanical rooms, water heater connections, and exterior architectural risers.
- **Cross-Linked Polyethylene (PEX-A / PEX-B):** Flexible, semi-rigid polymer tubing that revolutionizes residential repiping. PEX resists mineral scale accumulation and chemical corrosion, absorbs water hammer shock, and can be snaked through wall studs and ceiling joists with minimal drywall disruption. Its flexibility eliminates dozens of buried elbows and solder joints, significantly reducing potential future leak points.

Repiping projects require plan review, permits, and rough/final building inspections conducted by the City of Fullerton Building & Safety division. Attending contractors manage all permit filings and stage city building sign-offs.

## Private yard supply lines and underground pipe repairs {#underground-pipe-repairs}

Underground Pipe Repairs address private potable water supply lines buried in yards, beneath concrete walkways, and across landscaping. Soil shifting, seismic ground settling, and root intrusion from mature trees can shear buried water lines, producing saturated lawn patches, sinking pavers, or bubbling water along driveways.

Technicians deploy non-destructive acoustic ground microphones and line locating sondes to trace the exact underground path of private supply lines before excavation. Damaged underground runs can be repaired via targeted excavation and compression splicing, or completely replaced using seamless roll copper or high-density polyethylene (HDPE) tubing. Always ensure that DigAlert 811 is contacted at [811](tel:811) at least two business days prior to any yard excavation to mark public utility lines.

## Water Main - Install, Replace or Repair {#water-main}

:::conditional
Municipal public water distribution mains are maintained exclusively by the City of Fullerton Water Division and are not private contracting repairs. Our referred contractors repair and replace private water service lines running from the street meter box into the building.

When a private main water service line running between the city meter box and the house foundation collapses, develops multiple subterranean pinhole leaks, or constricts with corrosion, Water Main - Install, Replace or Repair is required. Contractors utilize horizontal directional boring or traditional trenching to install a new, continuous 3/4-inch or 1-inch seamless copper or high-pressure poly service main, ensuring optimal incoming flow volume.

## Troubleshooting low water pressure and pressure regulators {#water-pressure-problems}

Water Pressure Problems in Fullerton homes typically divide into localized fixture restrictions versus systemic whole-house pressure drops:

- **Single Fixture Pressure Drops:** Low flow restricted to one faucet or showerhead is almost always caused by mineral scale clogging the fixture aerator, cartridge debris, or a partially closed angle stop valve beneath the sink.
- **Whole-House Pressure Drops or Spikes:** If water volume drops across all hot and cold faucets simultaneously, the primary suspect is the household Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV). The PRV is a bell-shaped brass regulator located on the main incoming water riser where the supply line enters the home. 

In low-lying Fullerton neighborhoods, municipal street pressure supplied by the city can exceed 100 PSI to maintain service to elevated hillside tracts like [Sunny Hills](/areas/sunny-hills-north-fullerton/). A failing PRV can allow dangerous high pressure into household plumbing, blowing out appliance hoses and causing loud "water hammer" pipe banging, or fail shut, reducing household water flow to a trickle. California Plumbing Code Section 608.2 mandates that household static pressure be regulated between 45 and 75 PSI; contractors calibrate or replace failing PRVs to restore safe, balanced pressure.

## Winterization {#winterization}

:::conditional
Freezing temperatures are rare in Fullerton and North Orange County's Mediterranean climate. However, prolonged winter cold snaps or seasonal property vacancies warrant proactive freeze precautions for exposed outdoor plumbing and irrigation lines.
:::

Winterization for vacant rental properties, commercial spaces, or vacation homes involves draining domestic water lines, clearing irrigation valves with compressed air, and shutting off the main water valve at the street meter box.

## Frozen Pipes & Drains Thawed {#frozen-pipes-drains-thawed}

During infrequent winter cold snaps where overnight temperatures approach freezing, uninsulated exterior piping—such as outdoor irrigation backflow assemblies, hose bibbs, and exterior tankless water heater connections—is vulnerable to ice expansion and burst joints. 

If an outdoor line freezes, contractors provide Frozen Pipes & Drains Thawed services, gently thawing the line using specialized electrical pipe heating clamps or warm air blowers before testing the system under pressure for cracked joints or split solder seams.

:::faq
Q: How long does a whole-house copper or PEX repiping project take?
A: For an average single-family home in Fullerton (1,500 to 2,500 square feet), a professional repiping crew typically completes the plumbing installation in two to three days, with water service restored at the end of each work day. Subsequent city building inspections and drywall patch repair typically require an additional two to three days.

Q: Is PEX tubing as durable and safe as traditional copper piping?
A: Yes. Modern PEX-A and PEX-B piping materials are certified under NSF/ANSI 61 for potable drinking water safety and have been installed in millions of Southern California homes for over two decades. PEX is completely impervious to acidic soil corrosion and pinhole leaks, resists mineral scale buildup, and offers superior freeze elasticity compared to rigid copper.

Q: How can I tell if my household water pressure regulator (PRV) has failed?
A: Common signs of a failing pressure regulator include loud shuddering or banging sounds in pipes when faucets are shut (water hammer), vibrating toilet fill valves, sudden drops in shower water flow when another tap opens, or leaking temperature-pressure relief valves on your water heater caused by excessive incoming pressure spikes.

Q: Does the City of Fullerton require a plumbing permit for repiping?
A: Yes. The City of Fullerton Building & Safety division requires a plumbing permit for all residential water line repiping, main service line replacements, and pressure regulator installations to ensure compliance with California Plumbing Code standards.
