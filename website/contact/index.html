Call for plumbing availability

Fullerton Plumbing Referral operates as a telephone-first contractor introduction desk for residential homeowners, landlords, and commercial facility managers in Fullerton, California. Our system is designed without web lead forms, email queues, or automated chat bots, ensuring property owners connect directly with real service dispatchers who understand local plumbing requirements.

Independent referral service. Calls connect to third-party providers; availability and pricing vary.

Our telephone routing system is currently undergoing carrier integration and live dispatch testing. During this preview period, telephone controls remain inactive and do not place live outbound calls. When operational dispatch launches, verified telephone numbers and Pacific Time operating hours will be published across this page.

What to prepare before placing your call

To ensure the fastest possible contractor match, please gather a few basic property details before contacting our referral desk:

  • Specific Address and Community: Provide your Fullerton street address, including your neighborhood (such as Sunny Hills, Amerige Heights, Raymond Hills, or Downtown Fullerton) and your ZIP code (92831, 92832, 92833, or 92835).
  • Property Type and Access: Clarify whether the location is a detached single-family residence, a condominium with an HOA, an apartment building, or a commercial establishment. In multi-tenant buildings, confirm who holds legal authority to authorize main shutoffs or exterior cleanout excavation.
  • Plumbing Symptoms and History: Note whether the problem involves one fixture or multiple drains simultaneously. If chemical drain cleaners were recently poured into the line, inform the intake dispatcher immediately so field technicians can take proper safety precautions against chemical burns.

Emergency municipal and utility contact numbers

This referral website is intended for private property plumbing services that can be scheduled and quoted by an independent trade contractor. If you face an immediate public utility emergency, contact the responsible public agencies directly:

  • Suspected Natural Gas Leaks: If you detect a rotten-egg odor or hear hissing near gas meters or underground piping, evacuate the building immediately. Do not flip light switches, light matches, or use cell phones in the vicinity. From a safe location downwind, call Southern California Gas Company (SoCalGas) emergency dispatch at (800) 427-2200 or dial 911.
  • Municipal Water Main Breaks or Street Flooding: For major water line breaks in public streets, broken fire hydrants, or municipal sewer main surcharges, call the City of Fullerton Water Division / Public Works emergency standby at (714) 738-6897. For routine public works questions during business hours, call (714) 738-6890.
  • Electrical Outages and Downed Power Lines: For electrical supply issues impacting water heaters or pumps, contact Southern California Edison (SCE) at (800) 655-4555.
  • Underground Utility Markout: Always call 811 (DigAlert) at 811 at least two business days prior to any yard excavation, sewer trenching, or water main replacement.

Preparing for a plumbing referral call

Can Fullerton Plumbing Referral provide an exact cost estimate over the phone?

No. Because our referral desk does not perform contracting work, we cannot quote repair fees. Plumbing costs depend on physical access, pipe material, code compliance, and diagnostic findings. All pricing is determined by the independent licensed contractor who inspects the property.

How quickly can a referred plumber arrive at my Fullerton property?

Arrival windows depend on contractor scheduling, current demand in North Orange County, and the urgency of the problem. Active leaks and complete sewer stoppages receive expedited routing priority from contractors when technician capacity permits.

Does Fullerton Plumbing Referral dispatch city municipal workers?

No. We are a private referral service. For issues involving the public water distribution main, public fire hydrants, or municipal sewer mains beneath city streets, residents must contact City of Fullerton Public Works directly.

Sources and limits

  • City of Fullerton Public Works Water Division — municipal water service, emergency standby line (714) 738-6897, and sewer main maintenance.
  • Southern California Gas Company (SoCalGas) — immediate natural gas emergency service and residential pipeline safety guidelines.
  • Underground Service Alert of Southern California (DigAlert 811) — California legal requirements for utility line location prior to excavation.
