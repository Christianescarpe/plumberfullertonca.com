Water Purification & Filters

Tap water delivered across Fullerton by the municipal water division originates from the local Orange County Water District (OCWD) groundwater aquifer blended with treated imported surface water from the Metropolitan Water District (MWD).

While Fullerton tap water meets all stringent state and federal primary drinking water health standards, its natural filtration through underground mineral strata imparts moderate-to-high dissolved mineral hardness (primarily calcium and magnesium ions). Water Purification & Filters provide property owners with customized solutions to reduce fixture scale encrustation, protect water heaters, and improve taste:

  • Whole-House Ion-Exchange Water Softeners: Traditional salt-based water softeners replace hard calcium and magnesium ions with sodium or potassium ions through a resin bead bed, completely eliminating hard scale formation. *Health Note:* Because sodium ions are introduced into the water supply, individuals on medically supervised low-sodium diets should consult their physician before installing a whole-house sodium softener or install an unsoftened bypass line for drinking water taps.
  • Salt-Free Scale Inhibitors (TAC Systems): Template-Assisted Crystallization (TAC) systems transform dissolved mineral ions into microscopic inert crystals that remain suspended in water without attaching to pipe walls or water heater heating elements, operating without salt bags or wastewater discharge.
  • Under-Sink Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems: Multi-stage filtration units combining sediment pre-filters, carbon blocks, and semi-permeable RO membranes to remove dissolved solids, chlorine taste, and trace minerals from dedicated drinking water faucets.

Water Treatment & Purification System - Install or Replace

Contractors handle Water Treatment & Purification System - Install or Replace, sizing whole-home ion-exchange softeners, carbon backwash filters, and conditioned scale prevention units. Installations include code-mandated air gap fittings on brine discharge lines and bypass plumbing assemblies.

Water Treatment & Purification System - Repair or Service

Technicians provide Water Treatment & Purification System - Repair or Service across established residential filtration units, replacing exhausted filter cartridges, servicing stuck multiport valves, clearing brine line clogs, and testing output water hardness.

Commercial and residential backflow prevention assemblies

Backflow Preventers protect the municipal potable water supply from dangerous cross-contamination caused by backsiphonage (a sudden drop in street main pressure) or backpressure (downstream pressure exceeding supply pressure).

The City of Fullerton Water Division cross-connection control program mandates approved backflow prevention assemblies for:

  • Dedicated landscape irrigation systems equipped with chemical injection or auxiliary pumps.
  • Commercial fire suppression sprinkler systems.
  • Commercial boilers, cooling towers, and food service beverage carbonators.

Contractors install Reduced Pressure Principle (RP) and Double Check Valve (DC) assemblies and provide mandatory annual testing performed by certified backflow prevention testers, submitting compliance paperwork directly to the City of Fullerton.

Sump pumps and subterranean sewage ejectors

While Fullerton's climate is predominantly dry, hillside homes in Sunny Hills or properties with sub-grade basements and depressed driveway ramps require dependable stormwater and wastewater evacuation. Sump Pumps Installed & Repaired protect below-grade structures from water inundation:

  • Groundwater Sump Pumps: Submersible cast-iron or thermoplastic pumps installed inside a sealed basin with float switches to collect foundation seepage and pump water away to exterior swales. Battery-backup auxiliary pumps provide vital protection during severe rainstorm power outages.
  • Sewage Ejector Pumps: Installed in basements or lower-level living suites located below the building's gravity sewer lateral. Heavy-duty grinder or vortex solids-handling pumps macerate waste and pump it upward into the main gravity sewer line, equipped with check valves and dedicated vent stacks.

Manufactured housing and mobile home plumbing

Plumbing for Mobile Homes in established manufactured home communities involves distinct technical standards regulated by the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) rather than standard municipal building codes:

  • Polybutylene (PB) Pipe Upgrades: Older manufactured homes built between the late 1970s and 1990s often feature gray polybutylene supply piping and plastic acetal fittings prone to sudden brittle fractures. Technicians repipe mobile homes using flexible PEX tubing designed for mobile home framing.
  • Specialized Water Heaters: Mobile home water heaters must feature specific HCD/HUD safety certifications, side water connections, and specialized combustible air intake kits approved for enclosed manufactured home utility alcoves.

Hydronic radiant floor heating systems

Radiant Floor Systems circulate heated water through closed loops of flexible barrier PEX tubing embedded directly within concrete slab floors or fastened beneath subfloors, warming rooms evenly through gentle thermal radiation.

Radiant Floor Heating System - Install

Contractors perform Radiant Floor Heating System - Install for custom luxury homes and additions, sizing high-efficiency condensing boilers, circulating pumps, and multi-zone digital mixing manifolds. Work includes pressure-testing PEX loops before concrete pour.

Radiant Floor Heating System - Repair

Technicians also provide Radiant Floor Heating System - Repair, utilizing infrared thermal cameras to locate sub-floor loop leaks, purging trapped air from hydronic lines, and rebuilding zone control valves. *Scope Note:* Electrical radiant floor mat installations are an electrical contractor trade discipline outside this plumbing directory.

Special plumbing trade requirements

Is a whole-house water softener necessary for homes in Fullerton?

While Fullerton municipal tap water is completely safe to drink, its moderate-to-high dissolved mineral hardness (calcium and magnesium) causes noticeable white lime scale on shower glass, spots fixtures, and reduces the efficiency of water heaters. A water softener or salt-free descaler is an optional comfort improvement that protects plumbing infrastructure.

How often does the City of Fullerton require backflow preventers to be tested?

Under California Title 17 administrative regulations and City of Fullerton municipal ordinances, all commercial and residential backflow prevention assemblies must be tested and certified annually by an AWWA-certified backflow tester, with official test reports submitted to the city water division.

Can a regular plumber service plumbing inside a mobile home?

Plumbing repairs inside manufactured homes require specialized knowledge of California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) standards, specialized fixture sizing, and mobile home water heater certifications. When calling our referral desk, indicate if your home is a manufactured or mobile home.

What maintenance does a basement sump pump require?

Sump pumps should be tested at least twice a year—especially prior to the winter rainy season—by pouring water into the basin to ensure the mechanical float switch activates the pump and discharges water freely through the exterior check valve. Sump basins should also be cleared of mud and gravel sediment.
