Plumbing services

Fullerton Plumbing Referral organizes residential and commercial plumbing trades into nine dedicated service hubs. Rather than forcing property owners to decipher technical trade jargon, each guide addresses a specific functional system within your home or commercial building.

Independent referral service. Calls connect to third-party providers; availability and pricing vary.

Whether you are coping with a persistent bathroom drain backup in Sunny Hills, investigating a suspected slab leak under an Amerige Heights foundation, or planning a full copper repipe in Downtown Fullerton, select the relevant service category below to review diagnostic workflows, municipal permit requirements, and common repair methods.

Key Takeaway: Plumbing problems fall into specialized trade categories: gravity drainage systems, pressurized potable water supply lines, natural gas fuel lines, and dedicated fixtures. Selecting the correct system speeds contractor dispatch and ensures appropriate diagnostic tools arrive on site.

Nine comprehensive plumbing service clusters

  • Drain cleaning — Fixture clogs, slow sinks, overflowing laundry standpipes, floor drains, and high-pressure hydro-jetting.
  • Sewer line repair — In-line video camera inspections, private lateral repairs, trenchless pipe bursting, and root intrusion management.
  • Leak detection — Electronic acoustic slab leak locating, thermal imaging, pressure testing, and diagnostic plumbing inspections.
  • Pipe and water line repair — Whole-house copper and PEX repiping, private yard service lines, low water pressure troubleshooting, and winterization.
  • Water heaters — Conventional storage tank repairs, high-efficiency tankless installations, pilot light troubleshooting, and expansion tank retrofits.
  • Fixtures and remodel plumbing — Toilet installations, kitchen faucets, shower pressure-balance valves, garbage disposals, and bathroom addition rough-ins.
  • Gas line plumbing — Scheduled appliance gas lines, outdoor barbecue piping, fire pits, and seismic automatic shutoff valve retrofits.
  • Commercial plumbing — Multi-tenant commercial retail centers, restaurant grease lines, industrial facilities, and backflow compliance.
  • Specialty plumbing — Whole-home water softeners, filtration systems, sewage ejector and sump pumps, and certified backflow prevention assemblies.

Technical standards across all Fullerton service hubs

Every service category featured on this website reflects current California Plumbing Code standards as administered locally by the City of Fullerton Community Development Department (Building & Safety Division). When you request a contractor introduction through our referral desk, your call connects directly with an independent licensed California C-36 plumbing contractor.

Before starting major repairs, licensed plumbers evaluate property conditions, confirm whether city building permits are required, explain available material options (such as Type L copper versus cross-linked polyethylene PEX-a), and provide transparent upfront pricing. Work complies with Fullerton municipal setback, backflow prevention, and seismic safety guidelines. Contractors stage required city building inspections and provide comprehensive written estimates before any demolition or repair begins. For operational policies and CSLB licensing guidelines, visit how referrals work and our about page.

If there is danger, use the utility line

For a suspected natural gas leak in Fullerton, get everyone out of the building. From a safe location, call Southern California Gas Company (SoCalGas) at (800) 427-2200 or dial 911. Do not operate light switches, open flames, or mobile phones near a suspected leak. This referral site is not an emergency dispatch line.

For water main breaks, street flooding, or municipal sewer overflows, call the City of Fullerton Water Division / Public Works emergency standby line at (714) 738-6897. For general public works inquiries, call (714) 738-6890. Those numbers are official municipal contacts, checked November 2026. They are not this website’s referral number.
